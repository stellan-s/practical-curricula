# Practical Curricula

Four focused, project-based curricula for building useful competence rather than collecting background knowledge.

| Curriculum | Level | Time | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Effective Learning & Spaced Repetition](learning-and-memory.md) | Beginner | 6 hours plus a 14-day trial | Build a repeatable system for understanding, retaining, and applying material |
| [Enterprise Architecture](enterprise-architecture.md) | Intermediate | About 29 hours | Produce an enterprise-architecture portfolio case for an anonymized SaaS company |
| [Systems Thinking](systems-thinking.md) | Beginner | 10–12 hours | Model a real system and identify a defensible intervention |
| [Beginner Electronics](electronics.md) | Beginner | 25 hours | Build and debug simple low-voltage circuits from schematics |

## How to use these curricula

- Work through one curriculum at a time.
- Complete the exercises instead of treating the resources as a playlist.
- Use a real subject, system, case, or circuit throughout.
- Keep the artifacts you produce as evidence of competence.
- Stop when you meet the completion standard; add more material only when practice exposes a specific gap.
