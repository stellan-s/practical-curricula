# Practical Curricula

Project-based learning paths for structured study and for staying engaged with my interests and hobbies.

| Curriculum | Level | Time | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Effective Learning & Spaced Repetition](learning-and-memory.md) | Beginner | 6 hours plus a 14-day trial | Build a system for understanding, retaining, and applying material |
| [Enterprise Architecture](enterprise-architecture.md) | Intermediate | About 29 hours | Maintain and deepen practical enterprise architecture skills |
| [Systems Thinking](systems-thinking.md) | Beginner | 10–12 hours | Build, test, and challenge a small model of a real system, then design an intervention experiment |
| [Beginner Electronics](electronics.md) | Beginner | 25 hours | Build and debug simple low-voltage circuits from schematics |
